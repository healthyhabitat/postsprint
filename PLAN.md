# PostSprint — Plan

## Problem

Makers ship features, then go quiet. Launch energy dies in a single tweet. They need a **ready-to-post 7-day sprint** about *this* release — not a marketing course.

## Audience

- Indie hackers / solo founders who just shipped
- Product marketers at small SaaS teams
- Builders who want build-in-public without blank-page anxiety

## Why $1 (for PostSprint itself)

- Impulse buy after a free preview of days 1–4
- Matches “ship small, charge something” energy
- Enough to validate WTP and cover Stripe fees
- Upsells later: multi-product packs, team seats, scheduled exports

## Competition

| Alternative | Gap |
|-------------|-----|
| ChatGPT prompts | Inconsistent; often meta “how to market” advice |
| Social schedulers | Empty calendar; no launch narrative |
| Agency / freelancers | Slow and expensive for a single changelog |
| Generic swipe files | Not about *your* product |

PostSprint wins on **speed + specificity + ready-to-post artifacts about THIS launch**.

## Monetization

1. **Free:** Preview days 1–4, LinkedIn, community note, hashtags; watermark; no Markdown; days 5–7 / thread / CTAs locked
2. **$1 unlock:** full 7 days, thread, CTAs, Markdown download, remove mark, 1 regenerate
3. **Later:** $9 “multi-release” pack, Notion export, Zapier

## GTM (first 7 days)

1. Dogfood: run PostSprint on PostSprint’s own launch
2. Post on X with a screen recording of changelog → sprint
3. IndieHackers “I launched” + ask which day feels weakest
4. Reddit r/SideProject + r/EntrepreneurRideAlong (value-first)
5. Soft PH once Stripe live + custom domain

## Metrics

- Landing → Create CTR
- Generations / day
- Unlock conversion (goal: ≥5% of generators)
- Day-1 posts that actually go live (ask in follow-up)
- Qualitative: “Did you post all 7 days?”

## Risks

- Stripe not configured → no revenue (mitigated by OPEN_ITEMS)
- Template copy feels generic → iterate from real changelogs
- Abuse / unlimited free gens → rate-limit later
- Users paste empty / vague notes → tighten validation + examples
